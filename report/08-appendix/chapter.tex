\section{Firmware}
\label{sec:appendix-firmware}

The state diagram in \cref{fig:jack-monitor-states} names the states and their conditions. This section explains what it adds to \cref{sec:following,sec:firmware}, and \cref{tab:constants} lists the thresholds and periods. In \emph{Detect Plug}, the monitor first measures the rails and then runs the plug check on the empty jack.

In \emph{Identify Network}, rails older than \qty{30}{\second} are measured again before the solve. A solve rejected because its pairs disagree is retried. A solve that finds no current anywhere means that all arms are open, so Confirm Plug checks whether a plug is inserted at all. Without a plug, the jack is empty again. With one, the jack is open, a plug with nothing behind it, and the remembered pedal is forgotten, since it may have been swapped at the end of the cable. Other is a network that matches no known pedal: no MIDI is sent, and the jack is solved again.

In \emph{Follow}, the bus from Classify enters the potentiometer when one arm is the wiper, the footswitch when only tip and sleeve conduct, and the rheostat directly from an end stop whose total changed by more than \qty{20}{\percent} since the pedal was first identified, which marks a rheostat behind a mono plug. All three are followed as described in \cref{sec:following}, except that the ring is also driven low for a tip-sleeve element behind a stereo plug. A closed footswitch reports position 1 and an open one 0, and the position of a rheostat comes from the drop across the pull resistor at the tip over a standard full scale. The ring is checked every \qty{100}{\milli\second}: the ring of a mono plug that left the sleeve, or the ring of a stereo plug that carries current, indicates a different pedal. An open jack is solved again when tip or ring conducts, and at the latest after \qty{1}{\second}.

In \emph{Output}, Map Value applies the range, inversion and drive curve of \cref{sec:firmware}. The value is sent as a MIDI control change with hysteresis, published to the web interface and shown on the LED. The LED is off while the jack is empty and dim white for an unknown plug. It shows the color of an identified pedal for \qty{1}{\second} once per plug-in, purple for an expression pedal, lime for a footswitch and yellow for a rheostat, and then the value from dark red through orange and yellow to white.
