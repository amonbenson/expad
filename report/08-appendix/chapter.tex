\section{Firmware}
\label{sec:appendix-firmware}

\scaffold{
  \textbf{Paragraph 1, state machine} (about 200 words, \cref{fig:jack-monitor-states}). Walk through the conditions the diagram only names; thresholds and periods not listed here are in \cref{tab:constants}. \emph{Detect Plug}: without a plug the tip switch rests on the tip and the pulls halve the rail; above three quarters of the rail counts as a plug (\cref{sec:following}). \emph{Identify Network}: rails older than \qty{30}{\second} are measured again first; a rejected solve (disagreeing pairs) is retried; a solve with no current anywhere means all arms are open, so Confirm Plug checks whether a plug is in at all: if not, the jack is empty again; if so, the jack is open (a plug with nothing behind it) and the remembered pedal is forgotten, since it may be swapped at the cable's end. Other is a network that matches no known pedal: no MIDI is sent and the jack is solved again. \emph{Follow}: the bus enters the potentiometer when the wiper is at the star point, a switch when only tip and sleeve conduct, and a rheostat directly from an end stop whose total changed by more than \qty{20}{\percent} since the pedal was first identified (a mono rheostat). A potentiometer is followed with its track ends driven (start low, end high) and only the wiper read; the position is where the wiper voltage lies between the end voltages (\cref{eq:position}); one end is re-read every \qty{25}{\milli\second} and its pull drop must stay the same, or the jack loses track and is identified again, as when the wiper leaves the ends. A tip--sleeve element is followed with the tip high and the sleeve low, a stereo plug's ring low as well, and only the tip read: a closed switch (the pulls split the rail) sends 1, an open one 0; three readings in a row between closed and open make a rheostat, whose position comes from the tip's pull drop over a standard full scale. The ring is checked every \qty{100}{\milli\second}: a mono ring that left the sleeve or a stereo ring that carries current means a different pedal. An open jack is solved again when the tip or ring conducts, and at the latest after \qty{1}{\second}. Every followed jack runs the plug check every \qty{50}{\milli\second} and returns to Empty when the plug is pulled. \emph{Output}: Map Value stretches the position from the jack's range, inverts it if set and bends it by the drive curve ($-1$ to 1) (\cref{sec:firmware}); the value goes out as a MIDI control change from 0 to 127 with hysteresis, to the web interface, and to the LED, which is off while empty, dim white for an unknown plug, shows a recognised pedal's color for \qty{1}{\second} (purple expression, lime footswitch, yellow rheostat) and then the value from dark red through orange and yellow to white.
}
