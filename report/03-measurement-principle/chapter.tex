\chapter{Measurement Principle}
\label{ch:measurement-principle}

The adapter has to recover from the contacts alone which network is connected and how far the pedal is pressed. This chapter models a pedal as a star network (\cref{sec:network-model}), derives its arms from pair measurements and classifies it (\cref{sec:pair-measurement}), and shows how a plug is detected and an identified pedal followed with single readings (\cref{sec:following}).
