\chapter{Results}
\label{ch:results}

The adapter was evaluated from the board upward. Signals were recorded with a Rigol MSO5104 oscilloscope and Rigol PVP2350 probes at 10X, compensated to the oscilloscope input \cite{tektronix2026probes}, and the board was supplied by a Rigol DP932E laboratory supply at \qty{9}{\volt} with a limit of \qty{1}{\ampere}. Raw data and further plots are collected in \cref{sec:appendix-measurements}.
