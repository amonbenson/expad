\section{Hardware}

\scaffold{
  \textbf{Paragraph 1, design decisions} (about 80 words). The hardware went through the proposal \cite{benson2026proposal}, the circuit of milestone~1, a breadboard prototype with an AD7718 and SN74LVC126A buffers, and the final PCB. \Cref{tab:design-decisions} lists what changed and why; the text only adds what the table cannot hold. Two points deserve a sentence each: the move from tristate buffers to analog switches was the deliberate change that cost a new schematic and layout, because the current in \cref{eq:pair} needs a known pull resistance; and the shared resistor per rail means only one contact per rail can be driven at a time, which is exactly the one-high-one-low pattern of \cref{sec:pair-measurement}. The fourth channel of each switch chip was free and went to the tip switch.
}

\scaffold{
  \textbf{Paragraph 2, the front end} (about 130 words, \cref{fig:frontend-final}). Walk through one jack: the 74HC595 latches the drive pattern and selects the switches of U15 (to R50 and the \qty{2.5}{\volt} rail) and U19 (to R54 and ground); each contact reaches its ADC input through \qty{10}{\kilo\ohm} and \qty{10}{\nano\farad}. The \qty{2.5}{\volt} rail is also the reference of both ADCs, so every reading is ratiometric. The pull resistance was chosen from the extremes of a \qty{10}{\kilo\ohm} and a \qty{500}{\kilo\ohm} pedal: with \qty{1}{\kilo\ohm} the current-carrying drop is \qty{208}{\milli\volt} and \qty{5}{\milli\volt}, still ten times the reading noise, while the position uses the span of more than \qty{2}{\volt} in both cases (full series in \cref{tab:pull-resistance}). When a drive changes, a floating contact charges through the network and the filter, and the firmware waits \cref{eq:settling}, between \qty{1}{\milli\second} and \qty{400}{\milli\second}.
}

  t_\mathrm{settle} = 2\,(R_\mathrm{net} + R_\mathrm{f})\,C_\mathrm{f}

\scaffold{
  \textbf{Paragraph 3, ADCs, SPI and board} (about 100 words). Both AD7718 run in ten-channel mode, each reading two jacks; AIN6 is grounded and AIN10 tied to the \qty{2.5}{\volt} rail on both, which makes a self-test of the conversion path. Every SPI and shift-register line has a \qty{47}{\ohm} damping resistor at its driver: following \cite{ti1998scaa031}, driver output impedance and series resistor together should match the line, which removes overshoot and reduces emission (TI's example uses \qty{56}{\ohm} on a \qty{75}{\ohm} line). The \qty{5}{\volt} LDO also supplies the four LEDs, whose brightness the firmware limits to 10 of 255, about \qty{4}{\percent}, because the bare LEDs without diffuser are bright enough. The PCB was manufactured by Aisler and assembled by hand; 15 test points carry the signals of \cref{ch:results} (schematics and photos in \cref{sec:appendix-hardware}).
}
