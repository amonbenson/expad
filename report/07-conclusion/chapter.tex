\chapter{Conclusion and Outlook}
\label{ch:conclusion}

The next step is to complete the evaluation: a sweep with precision resistors would measure the linearity, test the settling model at its limit and resolve the deviation of the total resistance of the EV-30. On the hardware side, pull resistors of about \qty{4.7}{\kilo\ohm} would raise the current signal of high-resistance pedals four- to fivefold while keeping a position span of more than \qty{1.2}{\volt} (\cref{tab:pull-resistance}). Measuring the rails in the background while a jack is empty would shorten the identification time.

A second step prepares the adapter for use on stage. High-resolution control changes, which add a controller for the least significant bits, controller 43 for expression \cite{midi2026controlchange}, would pass on the resolution the measurement already has. Settings in flash memory, controls on the device, a more robust WiFi link, the 5-pin DIN MIDI output of the proposal \cite{benson2026proposal} and a low-noise rectifier for both supply polarities would complete it as a device for live performance.

Overall, this work demonstrates that an expression pedal of unknown wiring can be identified electrically within a tenth of a second and followed fast and precisely enough for musical control, using analog switches, two shared resistors per jack and a common reference.
