\chapter*{Notice on the Use of AI Tools}

AI-based tools were used in the preparation of this work. Anthropic Claude Code \cite{anthropic2026claudecode} was used to assist in developing firmware components, mainly unit tests, and to generate TikZ drawings and Python scripts for plotting measurement data. It was also used to check grammar and spelling and to reword individual sentences. All AI-generated content was reviewed and verified by the author, who takes full responsibility for the final content of this work.
