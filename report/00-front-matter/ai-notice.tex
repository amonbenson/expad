\chapter*{Notice on the Use of AI Tools}

AI-based tools were used in the preparation of this work. Anthropic Claude Code \cite{anthropic2026claudecode} was used to assist in developing firmware components, mainly unit tests, as well as generating Python scripts for plotting measurement data and TikZ drawings. It was also used for grammar and spell checking, as well as reformulation of individual sentences. All AI-generated content was reviewed and verified by the author, who takes full responsibility for the final content of this work.
