\chapter{Discussion}
\label{ch:discussion}

\scaffold{
  Budget: about 260 words in three paragraphs, prose only, no new results (writing guide, section 3.6).

  \textbf{Paragraph 1, what was shown and its limit.} An unknown passive pedal can be identified in place with switchable pull resistors and a slow high-resolution ADC and then followed with one conversion per update: the adapter recognized every available pedal type and wiring within \qty{0.1}{\second} (\qty{0.3}{\second} in the worst case) and updated it about 188 times per second, far beyond the 50 to \qty{100}{\hertz} target, with a position noise below one MIDI step. The limit of the claim: the measurement yields the star equivalent, so the adapter recognizes classes of pedals and their wiring, not arbitrary circuits, and its thresholds were set on a handful of pedals.

  \textbf{Paragraph 2, the method.} The speed came from changing what is measured rather than from faster hardware, since the same ADC delivered 2.6 updates per second at first. Noise-derived tolerances let one solver handle low- and high-value pedals, and the host tests made each solver defect reproducible within seconds. The subtle defects (a poorly conditioned solve, a too narrow wiper limit, the plug-in transients) showed up in noise and repeatability observations, not in functional tests.

  \textbf{Paragraph 3, limitations,} each with cause and remedy, in continuous prose: accuracy and linearity were only checked against an ohmmeter, where one pedal's total deviated by \qty{11}{\percent}, possibly because of its minimum knob; occasional misdetections while a plug moves are recovered only after a few seconds; the rail measurement adds about \qty{214}{\milli\second} to the first plug-in after \qty{30}{\second} and to every unplug; two pedals on one ADC halve each other's rate; potentiometer pedals need a stereo cable, which is a property of the pedal; the settings are lost at power-off; the 7-bit MIDI value discards most of the measured resolution; and WiFi connections to one Android tablet still drop now and then, even after the captive portal reduced it.
}
